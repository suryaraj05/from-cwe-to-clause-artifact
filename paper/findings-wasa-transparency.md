# Finding: Absence of a public WASA specification

**Placement:** results / standalone findings — **not** only threats to validity.  
**Status:** working finding (reopen if official NHA text appears).

## Claim

As of our retrieval date recorded under source X01, we found **no publicly available official NHA/ABDM specification** for WASA (Web Application Security Assessment) that states, in citable clause form, milestone structure, OWASP scope, Vulnerability Closure Certificate, Safe-to-Host certification, or CERT-In auditor requirements. Descriptions of those elements circulate primarily on vendor and auditor marketing pages.

## Why this matters for assessment frameworks

A compliance assessment instrument that keyed obligations to WASA would fail its own traceability standard: readers could not check a clause. Production-gate security expectations that are *said* to govern access to national health data infrastructure therefore cannot be operationalised from a primary legal/technical text in the way DPDP Act/Rules or the ABDM Health Data Management Policy can.

## What we do instead

| Theme often attributed to WASA | Public instrument used | Source ID |
|---|---|---|
| Web security assessment scope | OWASP Top 10 (edition pinned when cited) | S01 |
| Security-by-design / access-related policy duties | ABDM Health Data Management Policy | R06 |
| Empanelled auditor requirement | CERT-In empanelment listings | S04 |

## Implication

The gap is not merely bibliographic. It is a **regulatory transparency** finding: actors building or assessing health software for ABDM-adjacent deployment may be expected to satisfy a security-assessment regime whose authoritative rulebook is not published for clause-level scrutiny. Our framework therefore treats WASA as an object of study and refuses WASA-keyed register rows until an official document is retrieved and version-stamped.
